You are here

See yourself on your own maps

A blue dot with a heading arrow shows where you are and which way you're facing — plotted right on the georeferenced PDF you brought. Recenter with a tap and read your exact coordinates any time.

Stay oriented

Your sheet, in the real world

A dim live street map sits behind your map sheet so you always know where it fits in the wider world — then disappears when you don't need it. Everything you've loaded works with no connection.

Make a map anywhere

No map yet? Build one on the spot

Map My Location stitches a fresh map of exactly where you're standing in seconds — topographic, satellite, or worldwide street — and saves it on your device to use offline.

Mark it up

Waypoints, tracks & measurements

Drop colored flag waypoints with names, notes, and photos. Record a track that keeps logging with your phone locked. Measure distance and area, and share or export everything as KML/KMZ.

Keep all your maps in one place — even huge 100 MB+ sheets. A "You're here" badge flags exactly which maps cover where you're standing, so you open the right one fast.
