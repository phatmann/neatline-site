Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Neatline is built to work entirely on your device. It has no account, no servers of ours, and no analytics or tracking. We do not collect your personal data.

What stays on your device

Everything you create and load in Neatline lives only on your iPhone:

  • Your location and heading, used to show where you are on your maps.
  • The GeoPDF maps you open or build.
  • Your waypoints, tracks, notes, photos, and measurements.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives it, and Neatline does not create an account or profile.

Location

Neatline uses your location to plot your position on your maps, to tag waypoints, and — if you start a recording — to log a track (including while your phone is locked, if you allow “Always”). Your location is processed on your device and is not transmitted to us. You can turn location access off at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Map tiles from third parties

Two features fetch map imagery over the internet when you use them:

  • Context map — the dim street map shown behind your sheet.
  • Map My Location — builds a topo, satellite, or street map of an area you choose.

To draw these, Neatline requests map tiles for the area you are viewing directly from the map providers — the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS), OpenStreetMap, and their tile hosts. Those requests necessarily reveal the approximate map area being viewed to the provider, and are subject to that provider’s own privacy practices. Neatline sends only what is needed to fetch the tiles; it does not attach your identity, and it keeps no account with these providers. If you never use these features, no such requests are made.

No tracking, no ads, no third‑party SDKs

Neatline contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third‑party tracking SDKs. It does not use the Advertising Identifier and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Neatline is a general‑audience mapping tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes
